Loading settings…

Files and deployment settings stay on the host. Container startup copies local files into a private snapshot; a web-worker reload cannot pick up later host edits. Restart the container to refresh staged files, and recreate affected containers when their environment changes.

Loaded values follow this order: built-in defaults, shipped config.yaml, local config.local.yaml, then supported environment overrides. The locations below describe the usual deployment layout, not paths observed on your host.
